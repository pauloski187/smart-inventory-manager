# Loom Video Script (70 seconds)

---

**[0-8s] HOOK**

"What if your business could predict exactly how much stock to order before you ever run out? That's what Smart Inventory Manager does."

**[8-20s] THE PROBLEM**

"Most businesses lose thousands of dollars every year from two problems: overstocking products that don't sell, and running out of products that do. Manual spreadsheets can't keep up, and by the time you notice a problem, it's already cost you money."

**[20-35s] THE SOLUTION**

"Smart Inventory Manager solves this with AI-powered demand forecasting. The system analyzes your historical sales data and predicts demand up to 90 days ahead, for every product category. It automatically classifies your inventory into A, B, and C tiers so you know exactly which products deserve the most attention and budget."

**[35-50s] KEY FEATURES**

"You get a real-time dashboard with revenue, profit, and stock alerts at a glance. Monthly reports are generated automatically with AI insights like margin analysis and underperformer detection. A color-coded alert system tells you exactly which items to reorder now, this week, or just keep an eye on. And everything is exportable to CSV."

**[50-62s] RESULTS**

"The forecasting model achieves under 20% error rate on predictions, which means businesses can reduce stockouts, cut dead inventory, and make purchasing decisions backed by data instead of guesswork."

**[62-70s] CLOSE**

"Smart Inventory Manager turns your sales data into actionable intelligence. If you want to stop guessing and start knowing, this is the tool that gets you there."

---

**Tips for recording:**
- Share your screen showing the dashboard while speaking
- Switch to the forecasting page during the "AI-powered" section
- Show the alerts page during the "color-coded alert system" part
- End on the dashboard for a clean closing shot
